Walk in My Shoes

A live role-play game about human rights, inclusion and the lives of Roma, migrant and refugee young people. Pick a face-down card, step into someone else’s story, and decide what is fair.

Facilitator = the host. Create a game, share the code, start the questions and see everyone’s results. Players join with the code.
